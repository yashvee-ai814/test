# Whiplash reforms reducing small bodily injury claims

**Source:** ABI (Association of British Insurers)
**Date:** 8 February 2026
**Type:** Industry report

Continued benefits from the 2021 whiplash reforms are visible in the latest ABI claims data, with small personal injury claim volumes down modestly year-on-year.

The ABI's analysis frames this as a partial offset to the broader claims cost pressures reported elsewhere in the market: while vehicle repair cost inflation continues to push up claims severity, the reforms are helping to hold down the volume side of small bodily injury claims specifically, which had been a persistent cost driver for the industry prior to the reform.

The ABI cautions that the offset is partial rather than complete, and that repair cost inflation remains the larger driver of overall claims cost movement.
