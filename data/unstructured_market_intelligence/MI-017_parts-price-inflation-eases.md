# Parts price inflation shows signs of easing

**Source:** Thatcham Research
**Date:** 6 May 2026
**Type:** Analyst report

After two years of steep increases, average vehicle parts prices rose just 1.2% in the latest quarter, the smallest increase since 2021, according to Thatcham Research's latest benchmarking data.

Thatcham suggests this moderation, if sustained, could mark a turning point after a prolonged period in which parts and labour cost inflation was the dominant driver of rising claims severity across the motor market. The report is careful to frame this as an early signal rather than a confirmed trend reversal, noting a single quarter of data is not yet conclusive.

If the moderation continues, Thatcham suggests claims severity inflation for motor may begin to ease over the coming year, which would be a welcome development for insurers who have been repricing against sustained double-digit parts inflation.
