Increasing adoption of telematics ("black box") policies is helping some insurers offer more competitive pricing to under-25s while managing loss ratios, according to a widely-shared LinkedIn post from an industry pricing analyst.

The post argues that usage-based data - actual driving behaviour captured via telematics - allows insurers to segment risk far more granularly than traditional age-and-postcode rating alone. This lets telematics-enabled insurers offer meaningfully lower premiums to genuinely low-risk young drivers, rather than pricing the whole age band on its average risk.

The post generated significant engagement from pricing and underwriting professionals, several of whom commented that this dynamic is starting to fragment the young-driver market between telematics-based and traditional-rated propositions.
