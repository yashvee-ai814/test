# Hastings Direct undercuts market on comprehensive cover

**Source:** Insurance Times
**Date:** 22 January 2026
**Type:** News

Hastings Direct has been consistently appearing in the top three cheapest quotes on major price comparison websites for standard risk profiles, according to Insurance Times' regular PCW panel monitoring.

Analysts linked the pattern to Hastings' increased use of pricing automation, which allows faster and more granular repricing in response to competitor movements than firms relying on more manual quarterly rate reviews. The piece also points to lower acquisition cost ambitions at Hastings as a contributing factor, with the insurer reportedly willing to accept thinner new-business margins in exchange for PCW panel visibility.

The article notes that the strategy appears concentrated on standard risk profiles rather than higher-risk segments such as young drivers or high-performance vehicles.
