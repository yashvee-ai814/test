# FCA follow-up review of general insurance pricing practices

**Source:** Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) Publication
**Date:** 2 September 2025
**Type:** Regulatory

The FCA has published a follow-up review of the General Insurance Pricing Practices rules, which have been in force since January 2022. The review reiterates the core requirement of the rules: renewal prices must not exceed the equivalent new business price for the same risk.

Alongside the reminder, the regulator flagged that it continues to monitor firms whose renewal-versus-new-business pricing patterns fall outside expected norms, and said outlier firms should expect further scrutiny.

The publication does not name individual firms but is being read across the industry as a signal that pricing parity remains an active supervisory priority rather than a rule that was "settled" after its 2022 introduction.
