# Admiral cuts prices in bid for market share

**Source:** Insurance Times
**Date:** 15 September 2025
**Type:** News

Admiral has reduced premiums for younger drivers by an average of 6%, following stronger-than-expected claims experience in that segment over the past year.

The move positions Admiral as more competitive on price comparison websites for the 17-25 age segment specifically, an area where several insurers have come under pressure to hold rates up given rising claims costs. Admiral's pricing team is understood to have based the decision on internal loss-ratio improvements rather than a broader strategic repricing.

Brokers and analysts expect the move to intensify competition for young-driver business on the major PCW panels over the coming quarters.
