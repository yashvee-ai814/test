# Consumer group names and shames insurers on renewal hikes

**Source:** Which?
**Date:** 14 April 2026
**Type:** News

Which? has published a comparison highlighting motor insurers with the largest average renewal price increases over the past year, urging customers to shop around at renewal rather than accept an automatic rollover.

The comparison ranks major UK motor insurers by average logged renewal increase, based on a sample of quotes gathered by Which?'s own researchers. Several major insurers, including Aviva, were named in the mid-table - neither among the worst offenders highlighted in the report nor held up as an example of good practice.

The report is timed to coincide with Which?'s regular insurance-sector coverage and is likely to be picked up by other consumer-facing outlets in the days following publication.
