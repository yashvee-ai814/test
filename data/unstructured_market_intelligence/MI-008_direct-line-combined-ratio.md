# Direct Line Group reports improved combined ratio

**Source:** Insurance Times
**Date:** 18 November 2025
**Type:** News

Direct Line Group's latest trading update showed a combined operating ratio improvement to 96%, a notable step forward from prior periods.

The improvement is attributed to rate increases pushed through in 2024-25 now earning through the book, alongside easing claims inflation across the group's motor and home lines. Management commentary in the update framed the result as evidence that the group's repricing strategy over the past 18 months is now translating into underwriting performance.

Analysts covering the stock noted that competitors will be watching closely to see whether Direct Line uses the improved margin position to compete more aggressively on price in the coming quarters.
