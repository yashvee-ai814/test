# EV repair costs continue to outpace ICE vehicles

**Source:** Thatcham Research
**Date:** 1 October 2025
**Type:** Analyst report

Thatcham Research's latest repair benchmarking data shows battery electric vehicles are taking on average 14% longer to repair than equivalent combustion-engine vehicles.

The report identifies battery replacement costs as a major driver of total-loss decisions on EVs - in a growing number of cases, the cost of repairing battery damage after even a moderate collision exceeds the vehicle's pre-accident value, pushing the claim to a write-off rather than a repair.

Thatcham notes this is pushing up severity for comprehensive claims involving EVs specifically, and recommends insurers review repair network capability and parts supply chains for battery-electric vehicles as adoption increases.
