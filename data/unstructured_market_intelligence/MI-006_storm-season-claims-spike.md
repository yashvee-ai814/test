# Storm season triggers spike in weather-related claims

**Source:** Met Office / Industry Bulletin
**Date:** 20 October 2025
**Type:** News

A series of autumn storms across northern England and Scotland has resulted in a sharp rise in comprehensive motor claims for flood and falling-debris damage.

The bulletin, circulated jointly by the Met Office and industry claims contacts, notes that the worst-affected areas saw sustained high winds and localised flooding over a two-week period, with fallen trees and debris the most common cause of vehicle damage reported.

Insurers are bracing for elevated Q4 claims volumes as a result, particularly in the regions directly in the storms' path, though the bulletin stops short of quantifying the expected volume increase at a national level.
