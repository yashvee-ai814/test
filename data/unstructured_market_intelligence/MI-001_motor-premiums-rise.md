# Motor premiums rise as repair costs climb

**Source:** ABI (Association of British Insurers) Press Release
**Date:** 4 August 2025
**Type:** Industry report

The Association of British Insurers has published its latest Motor Insurance Premium Tracker, showing average premiums rose 8% year-on-year across the UK private car market.

The ABI attributes the rise to three compounding factors: increased repair costs, higher parts prices linked to import tariffs, and a shortage of qualified technicians that is extending average repair times. The combination is pushing up claims severity across the market, not just for any single insurer.

The tracker is published quarterly and is widely cited by pricing teams, journalists and consumer groups as the industry-standard benchmark for premium movement.
