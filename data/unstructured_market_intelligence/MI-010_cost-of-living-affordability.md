# Cost of living pressures affecting insurance affordability

**Source:** BBC News
**Date:** 14 December 2025
**Type:** News

Consumer groups report a rise in the number of drivers admitting to reducing cover levels or missing payments as household budgets remain squeezed, according to a BBC News report.

The report cites survey data from consumer advocacy groups showing that a growing minority of motorists are trading down from comprehensive to third-party-only cover, or in some cases lapsing cover altogether, purely on affordability grounds rather than any change in how they use their vehicle.

Consumer groups quoted in the piece raised concerns about underinsurance across the motor market as a knock-on effect, warning that drivers cutting cover to save money may be left significantly exposed in the event of an accident.
