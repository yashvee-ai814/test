# LV= expands telematics-based young driver product

**Source:** Insurance Times
**Date:** 19 March 2026
**Type:** News

LV= has announced an expansion of its telematics proposition targeting the 17-25 segment, offering premium discounts of up to 20% for demonstrated safe driving.

The expansion broadens eligibility for LV='s existing black-box product beyond its original pilot regions to a national rollout, with the insurer citing strong claims performance among enrolled young drivers as the basis for the wider discount tier. LV= positioned the move explicitly as a young-driver acquisition play.

The announcement is intensifying competition in a segment already under pricing pressure from multiple angles, with analysts noting that telematics-based propositions are increasingly the primary battleground for young-driver market share rather than headline rate alone.
